Orcas in the Antarctic Peninsula have a hunting technique that looks like teamwork from a movie: they line up, charge an ice floe and dive at the last second, creating a wave that washes seals off the ice.

It's culture, not instinct. Only some pods do it, and young orcas take years to learn.
