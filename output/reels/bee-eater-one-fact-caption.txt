A wild European bee-eater lands with a bee in Pfyn-Finges, Switzerland, and gets to work.

Bee-eaters really do eat bees. Before swallowing a stinging insect, the bird hits it against its perch to pull the sting out, then tosses it up and gulps it down.

Source: Animal Diversity Web, Merops apiaster.
