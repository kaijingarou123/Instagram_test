A wild herd crossing in Hoollongapar Gibbon Sanctuary, Assam, with the youngest one staying close.

Asian elephant herds are family groups led by an older female, the matriarch. Calves stay near their mothers for years and the whole herd slows down for them.

Source: IUCN Red List, Elephas maximus.
