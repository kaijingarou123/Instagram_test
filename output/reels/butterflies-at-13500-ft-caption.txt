Eight butterflies filmed near Sela Pass in Arunachal Pradesh, one of the highest roads in India.

At this height butterflies bask on warm rocks and soil to heat their flight muscles before they can fly.

Source: Sela Pass and Himalayan butterfly records, Wikipedia.
