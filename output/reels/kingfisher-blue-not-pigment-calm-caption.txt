That famous kingfisher blue (Alcedo atthis) is made by structure, not by colour pigment.

Electron microscopy shows spongy nanostructures in the barbs of its back and tail feathers. They scatter blue light and shimmer as the angle changes. The orange breast is the one part coloured the usual way, by small pigment granules.

Source: Stavenga et al. 2011, Journal of Experimental Biology.
Save it for the next time you spot one by the water.
