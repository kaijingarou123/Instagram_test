Same as yours: a giraffe (Giraffa camelopardalis) carries its neck on 7 vertebrae.

Height comes from length, not number. Each of those seven bones is greatly elongated, and long spines on the upper back anchor the muscles that hold everything up. With a tongue close to 0.5 m it strips leaves around 6 m above the ground, unbothered by thorns. Wild animal, Malawi.

Source: Encyclopaedia Britannica.
