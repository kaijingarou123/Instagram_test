Alcedo atthis, the common kingfisher, has no blue dye in its feathers.

Look closely and the blue is architecture: a sponge-like nanostructure inside each barb scatters light back as cyan on the back and deeper blue on the tail, shifting with the viewing angle. Only the orange underparts get their colour from actual pigment grains.

Source: Stavenga and colleagues, 2011, Journal of Experimental Biology.
