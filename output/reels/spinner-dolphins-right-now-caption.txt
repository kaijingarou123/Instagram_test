A pod of spinner dolphins in the Indian Ocean, seen from above.

Spinner dolphins rest in calm, shallow bays by day and head out to hunt at night. Their name comes from the way they leap and spin in the air.

Source: NOAA Fisheries, spinner dolphin species profile.
