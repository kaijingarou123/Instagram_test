The leopard (Panthera pardus) is a big cat that stores its meals in trees.

An agile climber, it drags kills up into the branches, out of reach of scavengers, and may go on hunting even while several carcasses are already cached. Its preferred prey weighs about 10 to 40 kg. This one was filmed in Rajaji Tiger Reserve, Uttarakhand, India.
