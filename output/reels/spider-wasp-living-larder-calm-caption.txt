The tarantula in this clip is alive, just paralysed by a spider wasp (Pompilidae).

Her venom goes into the nerve ganglia beneath its thorax. She then pulls the spider into a burrow, lays one egg on it and seals the entrance, so her larva has a living meal when it hatches. Britannica counts about 5,000 spider wasp species.

Source: Encyclopaedia Britannica, Spider wasp.
