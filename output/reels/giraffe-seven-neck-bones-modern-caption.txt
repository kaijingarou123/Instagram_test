Giraffa camelopardalis: its whole long neck is built from just 7 bones.

That is the standard mammal count, the same as a mouse, a whale or you. Evolution lengthened each vertebra instead of adding more, and big muscles along the upper back carry the load. A half-metre tongue then pulls foliage from around 6 m up. Recorded in the wild in Malawi.

Source: Encyclopaedia Britannica.
