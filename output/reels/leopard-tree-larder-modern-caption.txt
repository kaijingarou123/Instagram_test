Panthera pardus, the leopard, stashes food high up.

Climbing is its superpower: a carcass gets dragged up a trunk and wedged in a fork, safe from thieves below. A leopard with meals already put away may still go out and make another kill. Most of what it eats weighs 10 to 40 kg. This one was walking through Rajaji Tiger Reserve in Uttarakhand.
