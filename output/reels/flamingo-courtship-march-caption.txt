Lesser flamingos in a courtship display.

They court in big groups: hundreds march side by side, flag their heads and flash their wings, and pairs form inside the crowd. A bigger group display seems to help the whole colony breed at the same time.

Source: Animal Diversity Web, Phoeniconaias minor.
