A common kingfisher (Alcedo atthis), waiting.

That blue is not pigment. Spongy nanostructures in its feathers scatter blue light, so the colour shifts as it turns. Only the orange chest is coloured the usual way.

Source: Stavenga et al. 2011, Journal of Experimental Biology.
