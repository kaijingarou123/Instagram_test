A wild brown bear fishing for salmon at Crescent Lake, Alaska.

Rangers call this move dash and grab: the bear chases a salmon through the shallows and pins it to the river bottom with its paws. On the Brooks River in Katmai, a big male can catch and eat more than 30 salmon in a day.

Source: US National Park Service, Katmai brown bear FAQ.
