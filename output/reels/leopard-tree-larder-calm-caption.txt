A tree is the leopard's (Panthera pardus) safest pantry.

After a kill, this agile climber pulls the carcass up into the branches, away from scavengers that would steal it on the ground. With food cached, a leopard may still keep hunting. Typical prey weighs 10 to 40 kg. Footage from Rajaji Tiger Reserve, Uttarakhand, India.

Source: Britannica, Leopard; Animal Diversity Web.
