Five wild pollinators at work: a hummingbird hawk-moth in Tyrol, Austria, a bumblebee in Ohio, USA, a honey bee in Tokyo, a hoverfly in Hesse, Germany, and a snowberry clearwing moth on the same Ohio teasel as the bumblebee.

More than 75 percent of the flowering plants on Earth depend on pollinators. While they drink nectar, pollen sticks to their bodies and rides along to the next flower.

Source: US National Park Service, Pollinators.
