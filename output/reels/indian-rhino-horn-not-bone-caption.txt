No bone on this nose: the greater one-horned rhinoceros (Rhinoceros unicornis) grows its horn from keratin.

That is the same protein in your hair and fingernails. Scans of rhino horn show tiny keratin tubes packed in a keratin matrix, without any bony core, and a dense centre reinforced by calcium and melanin. The species is listed as Vulnerable on the IUCN Red List (assessed 2018), with numbers increasing. Filmed in Manas National Park, Assam, India.

Source: Hieronymus, Witmer and Ridgely 2006, Journal of Morphology; Animal Diversity Web; IUCN Red List.
