King cobras (genus Ophiophagus) are snakes that build nests, and they eat almost nothing but other snakes.

The genus name fits: ophiophagous means snake-eating. Since 2024, scientists recognise four king cobra species, so the big snake of northern and eastern India is Ophiophagus hannah, while the Western Ghats king cobra is O. kaalinga. A king cobra's hiss is unusually low, closer to a growl, and in a threat display it can lift the front of its body about a metre off the ground.
