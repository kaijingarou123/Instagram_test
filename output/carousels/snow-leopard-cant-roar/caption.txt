The snow leopard (Panthera uncia) is a big cat that does not roar. It yowls, chuffs and stays out of sight.

Snow leopards share the partly elastic hyoid bone that was long thought to explain roaring, yet their vocal folds differ from those of lions and tigers. Between 1986 and 2017 the species was listed as Endangered, then moved to Vulnerable after a calculation error was found in the 2008 population assessment.
