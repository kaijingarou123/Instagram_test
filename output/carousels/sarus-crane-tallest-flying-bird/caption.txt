The sarus crane (Antigone antigone) is considered the tallest flying bird, standing about 1.8 m.

Its bare red head and upper neck sit on a pale grey body, so a pair is easy to pick out across a field. Breeding pairs prefer to forage in natural wetlands, but they also feed in wet rice and wheat crops and take small aquatic animals.
