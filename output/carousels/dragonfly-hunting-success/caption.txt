Dragonflies (Anisoptera) catch prey in up to 97% of chases. The Blue Dasher (Pachydiplax longipennis) set that mark.

There are roughly 3,000 dragonfly species, and Britannica notes they sometimes take prey weighing 60% as much as they do. The 97% figure comes from greenhouse trials with fruit flies; against bigger, faster deerflies, success dropped as low as 20%.
