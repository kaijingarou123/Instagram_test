India's 'blue bull', the nilgai (Boselaphus tragocamelus), is not a bull at all. It is an antelope.

Even its scientific name is a mix-up: it borrows the Latin for ox and Greek words for deer, goat and camel. Nilgai were also released in Texas about 1930 and still live on large ranches there.
