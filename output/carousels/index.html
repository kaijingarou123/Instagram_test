claudes_plan101Queue #6chameleon-colour-myth
1/7
claudes_plan101 The panther chameleon (Furcifer pardalis) doesn't change colour mainly to hide. Its big colour switches are social signals.
more
Males flip from green to yellow or orange during contests and courtship by changing the spacing of a lattice of tiny guanine crystals in their skin. The colour comes from structure, not from moving pigment. The panther chameleon studied comes from Madagascar, and the same two-layer skin turned up in three other chameleon species.

Source: Teyssier et al. 2015, Nature Communications; Stuart-Fox & Moussalli 2008, PLoS Biology.
