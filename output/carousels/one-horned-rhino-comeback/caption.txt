Around 1900, Kaziranga had about 12 greater one-horned rhinos (Rhinoceros unicornis). Today it has an estimated 2,613.

Nepal went through the same collapse and recovery. Chitwan's rhinos fell to 60–80 animals in the late 1960s, then climbed back past 600 by 2015. Rhinos from recovered parks have since been moved to reserves where the species had died out.
