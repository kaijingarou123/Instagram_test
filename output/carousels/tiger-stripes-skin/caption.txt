Shave a tiger (Panthera tigris) and the stripes are still there: the pattern is in the skin, and no two tigers share one.

That uniqueness is a research tool. Since the 1990s, camera traps in India have photographed passing tigers and matched their stripes to tell individuals apart, turning photos into population estimates. The tigers in these photos were all photographed in the wild, mostly in Ranthambore National Park, Rajasthan.
