The grey-headed flying fox (Pteropus poliocephalus) can travel an estimated 2,500 km a year, carrying pollen and seeds.

This Australian fruit bat feeds on eucalyptus nectar and rainforest fruit, sometimes 50 km from its roost in a single night. Satellite tags on 109 of them logged 546 different roost sites across eastern Australia. In a Malaysian durian orchard, a close relative, the island flying fox, turned out to raise the number of fruits that ripen.
