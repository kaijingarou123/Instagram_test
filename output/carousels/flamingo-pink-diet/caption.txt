American flamingo (Phoenicopterus ruber) chicks hatch grey. The famous pink arrives years later, straight from their food.

Carotenoid pigments made by algae pass up through brine shrimp into the birds, so the shade depends on what each flock eats. One more surprise: what looks like a backward-bending knee is actually the ankle. The real knee is hidden up under the feathers.
