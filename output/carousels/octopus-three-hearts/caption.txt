The big blue octopus (Octopus cyanea) runs on three hearts and copper-based blue blood, with most of its neurons in its arms.

Also called the day octopus, it lives on coral reefs from Hawaii to the Red Sea at depths of about 1 to 100 m. Its name means dark blue, after the dark blue rings on its skin. Every photo here was taken on a reef at Nosy Tanikely, Madagascar.
