Only about 50,000 Asian elephants (Elephas maximus) remain in the wild, and IUCN lists the species as Endangered.

The 2018 estimate of 48,323–51,680 is uncertain: elephants are hard to count in dense forest, and some national figures rest on interviews rather than surveys. The Sumatran subspecies is Critically Endangered. All photos were taken in Sri Lankan and Indian national parks; we left out photos of working and camp elephants.
