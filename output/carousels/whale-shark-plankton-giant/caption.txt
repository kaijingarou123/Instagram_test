The whale shark (Rhincodon typus) is the largest fish alive, and it feeds by straining plankton from the sea.

It swims with its mouth open and lets its gills do the sieving, catching prey as small as 1 mm. Plankton is not the whole menu: whale sharks also take sardines, anchovies, mackerels, squid and even small tuna. Sometimes one feeds hanging tail down, bobbing at the surface so water and food flood into its mouth.
