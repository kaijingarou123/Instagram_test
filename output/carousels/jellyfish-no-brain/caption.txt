No brain, no heart, no blood: moon jellies (Aurelia aurita) and their relatives are about 95% water.

Instead of a brain, a nerve net runs through the outer layer, the most basic nervous system known in a multicellular animal.One relative goes further. The tiny hydrozoan Turritopsis dohrnii can revert to a polyp after reproducing; its close cousin Turritopsis rubra cannot, and comparing their genomes points to differences in DNA repair and telomere-maintenance genes. The jellies in these photos are moon jellies (Aurelia aurita) and other species, not Turritopsis.
