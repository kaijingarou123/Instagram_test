The killer whale or orca (Orcinus orca) is the largest member of the dolphin family, despite its name.

The name likely comes from sailors who watched orcas hunt large marine animals and called them 'whale killers', which later flipped to 'killer whale'. Wild orcas are found in every ocean, from the polar ice to the equator.
