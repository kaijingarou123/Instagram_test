The Arctic tern (Sterna paradisaea) weighs under 125 g and flies an average of 70,900 km every year.

Eleven terns carried 1.4 g geolocators for the study. South of the Cape Verde Islands their routes split: seven followed the coast of Africa and four crossed the Atlantic to follow the coast of Brazil. The oldest Arctic tern on record was at least 34 years old when it was recaptured in Maine.
