The gharial (Gavialis gangeticus) is a fish-eating crocodile with an estimated 650 adults left in the wild.

Its range once ran through the rivers of six countries, from Pakistan to Myanmar. Today wild gharials survive in fragmented stretches of river, mainly in northern India and Nepal, and the species is Critically Endangered. Females dig their nests in sandbanks in the dry season and lay an average of 40 eggs.
