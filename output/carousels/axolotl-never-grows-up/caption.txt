The axolotl (Ambystoma mexicanum) never grows up, and it can regrow its own limbs, spinal cord and parts of its brain.

It keeps its larval gills for life, carries a genome ten times the size of ours, and in the wild survives in exactly one place: the canals of Xochimilco, Mexico City, where it is Critically Endangered.
