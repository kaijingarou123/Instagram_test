Most monarch butterflies (Danaus plexippus) live a few weeks, but the last generation of summer can live six to nine months.

That long-lived generation makes the migration. Monarchs west of the Rocky Mountains fly a shorter route, about 500 to 1,600 km, to hundreds of groves along the California coast, where they cluster in eucalyptus, Monterey pine and Monterey cypress trees. In spring the journey north is a relay, taking 3 to 4 generations to reach the northern US and Canada.
