A male emperor penguin (Aptenodytes forsteri) fasts for roughly 4 months to keep one egg warm through the Antarctic winter.

While he waits, the female travels some 80 to 160 km from the colony to the sea to feed. To lose less heat he stands on just his heels and tail, so the soles of his feet stay off the ice. Chicks hatch in August, when the females come back to take over.
