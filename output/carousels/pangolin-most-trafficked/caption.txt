Pangolins are the only mammals with scales, and the most trafficked mammals on Earth.

No teeth, a tongue up to 40 cm, and a stomach that grinds ants with swallowed stones. Eight species; three are Critically Endangered, and international commercial trade in all of them has been banned under CITES Appendix I since 2017.
