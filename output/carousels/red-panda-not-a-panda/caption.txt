The red panda (Ailurus fulgens) is not a bear, and it is not a close relative of the giant panda either.

Scientists filed it with raccoons in 1825, then with bears, before genetic work placed it alone in the family Ailuridae. It weighs just 3–6.2 kg, and in India it is the official animal of Sikkim. What it does share with the giant panda is a wrist bone that works like a thumb.
