Every wild Asiatic lion (Panthera leo, Asiatic subpopulation) lives in one corner of India: Gir and Saurashtra, Gujarat.

The May 2025 count found 891 lions, up from 674 in 2020 and 284 in 1990. One of the newest homes is Barda Wildlife Sanctuary, 192 km² across Porbandar and Devbhumi Dwarka, where lions arrived on their own in 2023 and now number 17, including 11 cubs.
