The western honey bee (Apis mellifera) gives directions to food by dancing, encoding both direction and distance.

Karl von Frisch decoded it and shared the 1973 Nobel Prize for the work. Even when clouds hide the sun, bees can still read its position from polarised ultraviolet light. Decades later, radar tracking confirmed that recruits really do use the dance to fly to the food.
