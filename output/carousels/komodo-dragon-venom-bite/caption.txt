The Komodo dragon (Varanus komodoensis) is the largest living lizard, and its bite delivers venom.

It lives on Komodo and a few neighbouring islands in Indonesia. The 2009 study found a relatively weak skull and a low bite force, and pointed to its serrated teeth and its venom instead. At times it can smell carrion up to 4 km away, and an adult can eat up to 80 percent of its own body weight in one meal.
